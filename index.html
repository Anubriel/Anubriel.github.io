The Agrotech Problem

In elite Malaysian Tenera oil palms (Elaeis guineensis), tracking the growth rate of the Fresh Fruit Bunch (FFB) is crucial. Planters must determine the precise peak growth windows to schedule high-potassium fertigation, and predict the exact plateau indicating peak oil synthesis for harvesting.

By modeling the growth curve as a continuous function $f(x)$ over $20$ weeks of development post-anthesis (flowering), we use the first derivative $f'(x)$ to compute the instantaneous growth rate (kg/week).

$\int$ Mathematical Formulations

Cumulative Bunch Weight Function: $f(x)$

$$f(x) = -0.004x^3 + 0.12x^2 + 0.1x + 1.0$$

Where $x$ is weeks after anthesis ($0 \le x \le 20$), and $f(x)$ is the cumulative bunch weight in kilograms (kg).

Instantaneous Growth Rate (Derivative): $f'(x)$

$$f'(x) = \frac{df}{dx} = -0.012x^2 + 0.24x + 0.1$$

Calculates the immediate weight gain rate in kg per week at any precise instant in time.
